SOP & Proof Hub

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how SOP & Proof Hub handles information when you use the app. The current version is a device-local incident documentation tool. It does not provide user accounts, team cloud storage, or automatic synchronization.

1. Information you enter

You may enter an issue category, Order ID, store or hub name, incident date and time, and an optional description. You may attach an order screenshot and one or two supporting images. Those files may contain personal or order-related information, so include only information you are authorized to record and share.

2. Device-local storage

Reports and image evidence are saved in the app's local browser database (IndexedDB) on the device where you create them. The app does not upload these records to a SOP & Proof Hub cloud account or make them visible to colleagues. Clearing app or browser data, uninstalling the app, or losing the device may make records unavailable. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to local records. Protect the device with a screen lock and device encryption where available. Exported JSON backups contain report details and image data and are not encrypted by the app; store them securely.

3. OCR and PDF libraries

When OCR is used, the app processes the selected screenshot in the browser on your device. It may download the Tesseract.js library and English OCR language data from public content-delivery networks. PDF export may download the pdf-lib library from a public content-delivery network. These network requests can expose ordinary technical connection information, such as your IP address and browser/device details, to the relevant network provider. The app's OCR and PDF code is not intended to send your report text or selected images to those providers.

4. Sharing and backups

If you choose a share or export action, the app creates a report or backup on your device. If you use the operating system's share sheet, the destination app or person you choose receives the content you share and handles it under its own privacy terms. Check the recipients and attachments before sending.

5. Analytics, advertising and accounts

The current app does not include an in-app advertising system, analytics tracker, account registration, or automatic cloud synchronization. This may change in a future release; any material change to data handling should be reflected in an updated policy before release.

6. Your choices and security

You can choose what information to enter, export a backup, and delete individual saved reports from within the app. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock, keep backups in a secure location, and avoid recording unnecessary customer-sensitive information. Local storage is not a substitute for an organization's approved incident-reporting or evidence-retention process.

7. Children

This app is designed for workplace incident documentation and is not directed to children.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. For questions or to report a privacy concern, use the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post screenshots, Order IDs, customer information, passwords, or other private evidence in a public issue.
